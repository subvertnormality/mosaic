Before you explore Masks

Open Channel (grid 3,8), turn E1 to Channel tasks, choose Masks with E2 and press K3. E2 selects Trig, Note, Vel, Len or Chd1–Chd4; E3 changes the selected value. With no step held, E3 changes the channel default. Hold a step while turning E3 to set an exception for that step.

X means unset and inherit, not MIDI note 0. Masks labels MIDI 60 as C3; Fixed Note labels MIDI 60 as C5. Releasing a step returns to the channel-default view. Hold a step and press K2 to clear that step's overrides; with no grid key held, K1+K2 clears step overrides while keeping channel defaults.

01

A place to start

The scenes begin with a four-note C-major pattern on channel 1. On your norns, open the Channel editor with grid (3,8), use E1 to reach Channel tasks, select Masks with E2, and press K3. E2 chooses a mask; E3 changes it.

New to Mosaic? Install and choose a sound source, then make your first pattern. This pilot teaches Masks; the rest of the manual remains in README.
