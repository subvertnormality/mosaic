Mosaic page animation plan

This is a design proposal. The miniature artwork is being drawn; these cards describe the planned movements.

Each Mosaic-drawn page gets its own small, relevant character or object in the top-right corner. Movements are gentle and playful. The Doctor, plants and note creatures keep their original identity.

Icons use the spare title space, with compact versions where needed. Titles, scope, list rows, values and controls keep their space.

During playback, movement follows Mosaic’s beat. While stopped, it follows the selected norns musical clock, so the Doctor can animate during recording without starting the sequencer. Motion Off shows a resting pose.
